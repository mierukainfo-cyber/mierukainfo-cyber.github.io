Memory Match Quest Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

"Memory Match Quest" (the "App") respects your privacy. This policy explains how information is handled in the App.

1. Information the developer collects

The developer does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location. Your game records, level progress, and settings (music, background color, language, card design) are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC, to show ads. AdMob may collect and use information such as device identifiers (including the advertising ID), approximate location, and app usage to serve ads, measure their performance, and prevent fraud. This information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy.

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and Switzerland, the App asks for your consent to the use of this information when it starts, as required by law. You can change your choice at any time from within the App.

3. Children's privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

4. Sharing with third parties

The developer does not sell or share your information with third parties, except for the information collected by the advertising service described above.

5. Changes to this policy

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
